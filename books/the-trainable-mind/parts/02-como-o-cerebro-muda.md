This part changes the question. It is no longer "what does the tradition say"
but "what do experiments show". It begins by teaching how to read a study —
because nearly every exaggeration about meditation comes from someone who
skipped that step — and then goes through what is known about how the brain
and the body change: plasticity, mind wandering, stress, attention, emotion
and habit.

It ends with the balance sheet: what meditation science shows, how strongly,
and what it doesn't show. The answer is more modest than the magazine
covers', and more useful.
