This part brings the two before it together. Each chapter teaches a practice,
step by step, and says two things about it: what the tradition claims it does,
and what the science has found — including where the two don't match.

The order runs from the simplest to the most demanding: attention on the
breath, watching without grabbing, kindness and compassion, the body. Then
come the risks, which are real and have names, and finally what sustains a
practice over years.
