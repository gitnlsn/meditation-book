Before asking whether meditation works, you need to know what it is — and it
was born inside a tradition with its own diagnosis of human suffering. This
part presents that diagnosis from the texts, without the layer of self-help
that usually comes with it.

Four things are established here: who the Buddha was and what Buddhism
actually is; the problem it says it solves; its strangest and most debated
claim, that there is no fixed self; and the place of meditation within a path
that also includes ethics and understanding.

Everything in this part is what **the texts claim**. Whether those claims hold
up against the science is the subject of the next part.
